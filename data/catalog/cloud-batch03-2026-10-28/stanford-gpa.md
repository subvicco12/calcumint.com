Methodology facts: general grade points range from A+4.3 to NP0; L has temporary value2.0 from Autumn2014-15. GPA divides total unit-weighted points by included graded units. NP contributes units with zero points. CR, NC, RP, W, I, N/N-, GNR, S and other listed non-GPA symbols contribute no denominator. The official example B-/3, A-/4, B+/3 gives32.8/10=3.28. The A/4 and NP/4 example gives2.0. GSB equivalents for eligible undergraduate/graduate students are H4.14, HP3.54, P3.24, LP2.94, U0; MBA/MSx excluded.
