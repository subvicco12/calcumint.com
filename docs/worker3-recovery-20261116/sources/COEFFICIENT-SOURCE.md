# Supplementary source for normal-distribution coefficients

The original printed AMS55 page299, formula7.1.26, was retrieved as an image from the University of British Columbia mirror and visually inspected. All six literals and signs match the unchanged `normalTails` implementation. This is a primary printed source scan hosted by a university mirror, not an official publisher-hosted page. The separate official NIST landing confirms the publication identity but contains no coefficient table.

The printed formula is `erf(x)=1-(a1*t+a2*t²+a3*t³+a4*t⁴+a5*t⁵)*exp(-x²)+epsilon(x)`, with `t=1/(1+p*x)` and domain `0<=x<infinity`. Coefficients are `p=.3275911`, `a1=.254829592`, `a2=-.284496736`, `a3=1.421413741`, `a4=-1.453152027`, `a5=1.061405429`. The scan states `|epsilon(x)|<=1.5e-7` and attributes approximations7.1.25–7.1.28 to C.Hastings,Jr., *Approximations for digital computers*, Princeton University Press,1955(with permission).

The original scan is authoritative over OCR: OCR dropped the decimal in a2; direct image inspection reads `-.28449 6736`. Hashes, retrieval metadata, formula transcription and engine binding are in `coefficient-source-binding.json`. The JPEG itself is retained.

This evidence identifies the coefficient source. It does not certify the JavaScript implementation's global binary64 error, relative-tail accuracy, or application accuracy.
