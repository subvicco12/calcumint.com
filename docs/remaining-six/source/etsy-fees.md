To learn more about how VAT works on Etsy, see [this Help article](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360040584433?segment=selling).

### C. Taxes We Collect and Remit from Buyers

#### United States Sales Tax

In most US states, as applicable, Etsy will collect the sales tax amount from the buyer and pay this to the relevant tax authority. If the sale was made through Etsy Payments, the sales tax amount will appear in your payment account and is deducted from your current balance.

For items shipped to certain states, including but not limited to Washington state (or, for digital items, those purchased with a Washington state billing address), Etsy will use the category you assigned to each listing to calculate, collect, and remit the applicable sales tax based on the buyer's location in the US. For a full list of these states, please [see here](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000343968).

**Taxes Collected and Remitted from buyers outside the US**

Collection of VAT on Physical Goods: Read about Other Taxes we Collect and Remit from buyers in Canada [here](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/6633345416215-What-Does-Etsy-Remit-to-Canadian-Tax-Authorities-?segment=selling) and for other countries outside the US and Canada [here](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000337247?segment=selling#uk).

#### Digital VAT Fees

Under local laws in certain countries, Etsy is required to collect and remit VAT to the relevant tax authorities when you sell a digital item delivered via automatic download to the buyer. See [this Help article](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015587567) for further details. Etsy will automatically display the increased amount charged to the buyer of the digital item to collect the correct amount of VAT, and we will remit it to the tax authorities. If the sales were made through Etsy Payments, Etsy will collect the VAT amount from the buyer and pay the VAT to the relevant tax authority, and you will not have to take any further action. If the sale was not made through Etsy Payments, you will receive the additional amount paid by the buyer for the VAT, and you will need to pay this amount to Etsy. The VAT amount paid by the buyer will appear on your payment account and will be deducted from your current balance.

## 6\. Other Governmental Fees, Charges and Levies

Certain jurisdictions may require Etsy to collect other fees, charges or levies (e.g., environmental fees) from buyers and to remit those amounts to the relevant governmental authorities. Where Etsy is required by law to collect such amounts, they will be charged to buyers at checkout.

## 7\. Marketplace Reporting of Seller Information

Where legally required, Etsy will report your personal details and details of your transactions to relevant governmental authorities.

For US sellers, Etsy will issue 1099-K forms to certain sellers to comply with IRS and applicable state requirements. You may opt in at any time to receive your 1099-K form electronically. You can learn more in this [Help article](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000336447).

Etsy will not disclose any personal information to any third parties unless legally required to do so.

## 8\. Etsy Gift Cards, Etsy Credits & Etsy Coupons

If you are registered to use Etsy Payments, you may accept payment by Etsy Gift Cards, Etsy Credits, and Etsy Coupons. Etsy Gift Cards, Etsy Credits, and Etsy Coupons are subject to the [Etsy Gift Cards, Credits & Coupons Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/gift-cards). Etsy also charges the Etsy Payments payment processing fee described above on Etsy Gift Card, Etsy Credit, and Etsy Coupon transactions.
