# B11 — Launch Calculator Portfolio & SEO Certification

## Outcome
B11 expands CalcuMint from the two foundational public tools to a launch portfolio of at least 100 additional deterministic calculators across math, geometry, statistics, science and everyday-use categories. All launch definitions are standard-risk; finance, health and tax calculators remain outside this automated certification batch until specialist YMYL review is available.

## Publication contract
Every B11 launch calculator has a unique canonical slug, deterministic calculation function, finite-number Zod validation, formula disclosure, reviewed launch source note, golden default vector, explanatory content, assumptions, FAQs, keywords and related-calculator links. Public pages continue to be registry-gated: only `certified` definitions appear in static parameters, sitemap/category discovery and calculator content lookup.

## UI
A generic metadata-driven calculator interface renders each launch definition from its declared fields. Numeric output is computed exclusively by the deterministic engine. AI explanation is optional and receives the already-computed result; it cannot replace the numeric calculation.

## SEO architecture
The launch catalog creates distinct category hubs for Math, Geometry, Statistics, Science, Everyday and Unit Conversions. Each page has canonical metadata through the existing B2 route, breadcrumb structured data, formula, worked-example proof, assumptions, FAQs and two contextually related calculators. The sitemap automatically expands from the certified public catalog.

## Quality gate
Automated tests require at least 100 new calculators, unique slugs, standard-risk certification, finite deterministic golden-vector execution, and minimum SEO-content completeness. B10 remains the operational governance layer for future inventory/re-review. B11 intentionally avoids bulk-certifying YMYL calculators.

## Launch boundary
B11 is code-complete only after CI passes lint, TypeScript, all unit/security/portfolio tests and a production Next.js build. No merge to `main`, production Supabase migration, Paddle/AI/AdSense activation or Hostinger deployment is part of B11.
