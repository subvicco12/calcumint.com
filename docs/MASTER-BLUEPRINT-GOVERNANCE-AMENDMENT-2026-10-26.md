## Normative prospective review rules

1. **Assess the actual contract.** Record exact catalog ID, canonical mapping decision, implementation ID, technical version, full source SHA, selected formula/domain/units/timing/rounding, intended users and claims. Do not silently replace a medical model with arithmetic, a pension with generic growth, or a cooking-time calculator with a temperature lookup.
2. **Classify by actual risk and applicability.** Use the highest applicable tier below; no automatic downgrade based on a domain label, citations, passing arithmetic witnesses or an owner preference. Unassessed risk blocks review. A safer contract requires explicit scope/mapping selection; it does not retrospectively resolve the original identity.
3. **Use substantive authoritative evidence.** Record publisher, complete substantive text, exact clause/page, revision, snapshot SHA256, retrieval/review dates, applicability, exclusions and any conflicts. An accessible URL, bibliography, summary, abstract or internal golden is not automatically sufficient. Do not invent effective dates from publication dates, retrieval dates or URLs. Unavailable/conflicting governing text blocks the relevant claim.
4. **Prove mathematical behavior independently.** Authored independent expected cases, boundaries, units, finite-output/domain handling, explicit rounding/error tolerances and no-solution branches are mandatory. Verify headline/chart/schedule/scenario/export agreement, plan/mode parity and relevant UX/accessibility/security. A mathematically correct witness does not approve the selected model, clinical population or legal interpretation.
5. **Use competent reviewers with real identity.** A named independent technical reviewer must document relevant competence and exact evidence inspected; the author cannot be their own independent review. The reviewer may be an engineer for sufficient bounded/source-defined calculations. Required qualified external review must additionally document authentic qualifications, applicability/limitations and a decision bound to exact ID/version/implementationSHA. Automated agents can perform engineering review but cannot impersonate clinicians, regulated professionals or human qualified signatories.
6. **Retain law/contract/safety obligations.** An established external-review mandate applies at every tier. Unknown obligations cannot be treated as a negative finding. Unresolved clinical, food/physical safety or consequential predictive applicability requires appropriate qualified review. Disclaimers and source availability cannot substitute for that judgment.
7. **Require complete current official rules.** Tax, statutory/contractual financial, payroll and other rule-sensitive results require the selected jurisdiction (including region where applicable), rule revision, effective interval, tax year/currency when relevant, official clause-backed source, reviewed exceptions and jurisdiction-specific independent cases. Verify currency at the assessment date. The same source record must provide both the current verification and exact jurisdiction/revision/effective-period binding. Historical rule packs may be used only for explicitly dated historical calculations bound to the selected contract; a current check does not rewrite old snapshots. Future/unadopted, expired-for-target or jurisdiction-mismatched packs fail. Official formula correctness does not settle legal applicability.
8. **Separate review from lifecycle.** Review readiness permits preparation of a certification submission, never automatic certification. Keep draft→review→certified→published, active authenticated reviewer/QA authority, structured reviewed sources, revalidation triggers and owner/admin publication permissions. Publication additionally requires the existing exact ID/slug/version database manifest and certified repository definition. Reviewers cannot publish; draft/reviewed/private definitions cannot become public even with a forged matching manifest.
9. **Refresh prospectively.** Record next review triggers: amended rules/source, source conflict, altered population/claims, input/output contract, numerical method or dependencies. Each material change needs version-bound reassessment/recertification. Keep all previous evidence and outputs immutable. Historical pending specialist approvals stay pending; new engineering review is a distinct record, never retroactive specialist approval.
10. **No convenience waivers.** Missing evidence cannot be filled by unchecked booleans, fictional credentials, calculator-generated goldens or changing mathematics to satisfy the policy. The offline evaluator checks packet structure and consistency; it authenticates neither assertions nor evidence contents. Its references/hashes must be inspected by authorized reviewers before any real QA record is accepted.

## Risk tiers and minimum evidence

| Tier | Typical scope | Minimum review in addition to universal rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bounded-arithmetic | Explicit supplied totals/ratios/unit arithmetic; no hidden rules/advice | Primary/standard source or independent source-defined derivation; literal reference cases, units/rounding/domain; competent independent engineering review. |
| deterministic-model | Fully selected finite recurrence, discounted-flow/comparison, engineering scenario | Complete selected method and assumptions; primary methodological source, independent numerical/reference and no-solution/sensitivity cases; review applicability/limitations and escalate consequential claims. |
| rule-dependent | Tax/payroll, issuer/plan/provider contracts, official institutional or sporting rules | Complete official adopted rule source, jurisdiction/contract, effective revision/year, source hash, exceptions, independent jurisdiction-specific cases and current-source check. Engineering review is permitted only when applicability/interpretation is established. |
| safety-or-high-consequence | Unresolved clinical/population/training/food-safety or claimed financial safety model | Qualified version-bound applicability/safety/model review plus complete source/model evidence and independent engineering review. Source-only review cannot clear currently unresolved judgments. |
| unassessed | Unknown scope, applicability, obligation or harm | Block until assessed. Never default to low risk. |

The tiers are **governance review tiers**, not edits to existing `CalculatorRiskClass` strings or legacy lifecycle records. A healthcare-related operation may be low-risk only where the actual approved contract is pure arithmetic without unresolved clinical meaning. Conversely ordinary arithmetic with a binding assurance/reviewer obligation still needs that reviewer. An explicit reclassification record may be proposed when new authoritative evidence establishes applicability and safety; there is no automatic exemption or permanent blanket specialty label.
