# #214 Decimal to Fraction policy reconciliation

Evidence baseline: `cac3becb8957c10452b3a66e882b8a3a1d7ec555`. This record observes the legacy candidate `launch-decimal-to-fraction-calculator`, version 1. Catalog #214 has an exact observed binding but no approved canonical mapping. The supplied publication-readiness ZIP is bound by SHA-256 `dc6262919f06579e779cc59c4dfcda9f7db0bbdba2c56f586a81003577a412be`. Its CODEX44-02 depends on OC44-03 and competent numerical method decision. #214 is excluded from the selected first20 wave; this evidence preserves its pending work.

The displayed formula is `reduced round(decimal × 10^precision) / 10^precision`. Both compute and fraction formatter actually add the absolute `Number.EPSILON` to the input before scaling. For `{decimal:1.005, precision:2}`, literal JavaScript evaluation of the declared formula gives `1`, while the actual engine gives `1.01` and `101/100`. This is a concrete declaration/implementation discrepancy. The exact CODEX44-02 witness `{decimal:0.4999999999999999, precision:0}` gives literal declared result `0` but actual engine result `1`, formatted `1/1`; the new test asserts all three.

The retained rounding policy uses binary64 inputs and `Math.round` toward positive infinity at ties. For `-0.125` at two places, it gives `-0.12`, displayed `-3/25`; half-away-from-zero would give `-0.13`. For `-0.5` at zero places, the numeric output is `-0`, while fraction display is `0/1`. No evidence in the reviewed native Blueprint establishes half-away-from-zero as the governing policy. These examples distinguish policies without inventing an approved replacement.

The October 15 contract already documents these behaviors and explicitly requires a separate contract for exact-decimal input semantics, tie changes, arbitrary rational formatting, overflow rescaling or a new safe-integer restriction. All finite decimal Numbers enter the schema; precision is integer 0–12. Scaling overflow rejects in the finite-result guard. A blanket MAX_SAFE_INTEGER restriction would reject currently accepted inputs, including exact scaled numerator `9008000000000000` for 9008 at 12 places.

Native Blueprint BP-0139 requires explicit rounding as part of certification; BP-0101 calls for internal change identity for formula or rounding changes. BP-0194 requires cross-presentation agreement within the explicit policy, and BP-0429 requires methodology to retain rounding, revisions and references. Thus the missing decision is approval of the exact catalog mapping and full scope, followed by the pending OC44-03 decision on exact decimal versus binary64, tie mode, signed zero, precision limits and display/replay meaning. Passing EPSILON fixtures cannot establish an authoritative numerical policy. Retention needs authorized matching methodology wording; arithmetic changes need a separately authorized revision and compatibility decision. Existing candidate status and passing fixtures do not settle those decisions.

Existing references are ECMA-262 [Math.round](https://tc39.es/ecma262/multipage/numbers-and-dates.html#sec-math.round) and [Number.EPSILON](https://tc39.es/ecma262/multipage/numbers-and-dates.html#sec-number.epsilon), reused from the October 15 record, without new external retrieval or approval claims. The existing fixture literals provide independent expected values within their documented scope, not full canonical-domain acceptance.

No production source changed or full build ran. The companion JSON contains exact source hashes, paths, numerical witnesses and retained approval gates.

Replay and authenticated browser acceptance remain unproven by these bounded engine/formatter witnesses. No metadata alignment or arithmetic correction is authorized by this evidence.
