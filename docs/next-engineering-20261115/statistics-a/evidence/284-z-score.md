### _Z_-Scores

If _X_ is a normally distributed random variable and _X_ ~ _N(μ, σ)_, then the _z_-score is:

𝑧=𝑥–𝜇𝜎z=x–μσz=x–μσ

**The _z_-score tells you how many standard deviations the value _x_ is above (to the right of) or below (to the left of) the mean, _μ_.** Values of _x_ that are larger than the mean have positive _z_-scores, and values of _x_ that are smaller than the mean have negative _z_-scores. If _x_ equals the mean, then _x_ has a _z_-score of zero.
